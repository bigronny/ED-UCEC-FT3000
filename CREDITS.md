# Credits

UCEC FT3000 is built on other people's work. Thank you to all of them.

## Settlement schematics

- **CMDR Quizengine's Odyssey settlement maps** (OMG): the schematic plans were traced from these maps. They are now
  hosted by Elite Dangereuse: <https://elitedangereuse.fr/outils/quizengine/omg_1.1.html>
- **SrvSurvey**, by CMDR Grinning2001 (njthomson) and contributors: the settlements' measured positions, doors,
  terminals, loot and combat-zone points, matched to each plan. Licensed under GPL-3.0; each plan file says so in its
  `srvsurvey` block. <https://github.com/njthomson/SrvSurvey> (EDCodex:
  <https://edcodex.info/?m=tools&entry=590>)
- **Raven Colonial**: the settlement layout names and landing-pad counts. <https://ravencolonial.com>
- Settlement reference pictures, linked from the plan files: CMDRs Kekosummer and Grinning2001.
- Landing-pad sizes: the Elite Dangerous Wiki (Fandom). <https://elite-dangerous.fandom.com/wiki/Landing_Pad>

## System data

- **Spansh**: bodies, stations, signals and coordinates, from players' EDDN uploads. <https://spansh.co.uk>
- **EDSM**: traffic, deaths and exploration value (and bodies, stations and coordinates where Spansh has none).
  <https://www.edsm.net>
- How the sources are combined follows **EDDiscovery**. <https://github.com/EDDiscovery/EDDiscovery>

The FT3000 tells both services its name, version and this project's address, and asks only about the systems you're
in or look at.
