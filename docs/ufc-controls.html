WINWING UFC · GETTING AROUND

The default controls. Press ILS first: its menu works the FT3000's page. Any of them can be moved to another controller on 4 Setup > Controls.
1 Menu buttons · pick a menu
ILSThe page menu: the FT3000's tabs, DSG and MK on the option buttons
D/LNavigation: galaxy map, system map, next system, supercruise, jump

The other menu buttons press Elite's own keys (targets, wing, fighter, toggles); the README's table lists them. The option windows show the menu's five choices; the scratchpad shows what's going on.

2 Option buttons · the page menu

1-9 pick a page of the tab showing (on Syst, 1 again: list or map). ENT opens what COMM1 has highlighted (Wpt, Atk); CLR closes it.

4 COMM1 knob · the list

Turn: move the highlight through the page's list - contracts, bodies and stations, objectives, waypoints - or its switches.

Pull: select it, or press it.

5 COMM2 knob · the right-hand panel

Turn: scroll the right-hand panel, or step through the page's buttons.

Pull: press the button it's on.
